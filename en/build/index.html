How we work

  • Review with previews

    Every change gets a preview link you can open on your phone and comment on.

  • Screens checked by language and device

    We capture every language on mobile and desktop to catch overflowing or broken text.

  • Automatic checks before release

    Secrets, broken links and required tags are checked, and only a passing build goes live.

  • Mindful of running costs

    We suggest hosting and operations that fit the site, so you avoid costs you do not need.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take?

It depends on the number of pages, languages and features. Once requirements are clear, we propose a step-by-step schedule.

How many rounds of revisions are included?

The number of revisions and the review period are agreed and written into the contract. You review on a preview link and send comments.

Do you look after the site after launch?

Yes. We can take on operation and maintenance, such as content updates, new pages and regular checks.

What about the domain and hosting?

We help connect your existing domain or set up a new one, and suggest hosting that fits the site and keeps running costs low.

How much does it cost?

It depends on scope and features. We quote after a consultation.

Have a service in mind?

Tell us what you want to build, any reference sites and your target launch date. We will come back with a scope and schedule, in Korean or English.
