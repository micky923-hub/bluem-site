FAST Advertising

Together with K20, BlueM Partners sells advertising, channels and sponsorships on free ad-supported smart TV channels (FAST) in Korea.

What is FAST?

FAST (Free Ad-supported Streaming TV) lets viewers watch smart TV channels for free in exchange for ads. No subscription is needed: live channels and VOD play right away on any internet-connected smart TV.

Ad products

Home Screen

TV home-screen banner

A large banner that fills half of the smart TV home screen. It can be video or image, and links to a website or YouTube.

  • Fixed time slot (6 pm – midnight or 24 hours) or impression-based rotation
  • Minimum spend KRW 30 million (excl. VAT); fixed slots booked 3 weeks ahead
In-stream Video

In-channel video ad

A non-skippable video ad that plays within FAST channel content. You can target time slots and channels and see delivery data.

  • 15 / 30 / 60 / 120 seconds, HD (1920×1080) MP4
  • Minimum spend KRW 5 million (excl. VAT)

FAST channels for YouTube creators

We turn your YouTube channel into a free 24-hour channel on smart TVs. BlueM Partners guides you from the first conversation to the contract, and K20 builds and runs the channel.

Why it works for creators

  • Your videos work again

    We schedule the videos you have already published into a 24-hour channel. No new filming is needed to start.

  • Reach new viewers

    Meet living-room TV viewers who flip through channels instead of searching, outside YouTube recommendations.

  • Add a new revenue stream

    TV ad revenue from the channel is shared with you. It usually starts to build a few months after launch.

  • Become a creator with a TV channel

    Offer sponsors, brand partners and commerce partners the reach of a 24-hour TV channel.

A good fit for channels with a large video library and a clear fan base, such as hobbies, travel, fitness, cooking or gaming. Expanding to smart TVs overseas can be considered too.
