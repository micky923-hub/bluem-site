Together with K20, BlueM Partners sells advertising, channels and sponsorships on free ad-supported smart TV channels (FAST) in Korea.

What is FAST?

FAST (Free Ad-supported Streaming TV) lets viewers watch smart TV channels for free in exchange for ads. No subscription is needed: live channels and VOD play right away on any internet-connected smart TV.
