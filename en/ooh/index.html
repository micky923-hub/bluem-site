What BlueM Partners does

  • Media planning

    We propose a mix of transit, retail, entertainment and giant LED media to fit your goals, audience and budget.

  • Availability and booking

    We check open slots for your target month first, then handle the booking and order with the media owner.

  • Creative specs and review

    We send resolution, length and print specs for each medium and manage the media owner's creative review.

  • Delivery check and report

    We share photos of the posted or aired ads and the delivery data from the media owner.
